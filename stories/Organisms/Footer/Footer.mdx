# Footer

A page footer with a title and content on the left and links on the right.

<Controls />

## Basic Footer

The default footer shows a title and content on the left and a list of links on the right. On small screens the two sides stack.

<Canvas of={FooterStories.Default} />

Important Notes:

1. The `title` is required and renders as a level two heading.
2. The `links` are required. Each link needs a unique `id`.
3. Content passed as `children` appears below the title.
4. The footer uses the `dark` theme unless a `theme` is set.
5. The `copyright` is the name shown at the bottom, after the © symbol and the current year. The line is hidden when omitted.

## Content Variants

Set the `share` property to show a [Share](?path=/docs/engine-ds-atoms-share--docs) below the content. It accepts the same properties as the Share itself.
