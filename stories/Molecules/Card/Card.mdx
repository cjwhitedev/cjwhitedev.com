# Card

A teaser style element ment to link to more information. The card will grow to fit its wrapper.

It fades in as it scrolls into view. Set `animate` to `false` to turn that
off. See Motion for details.

## Use Case Contrast

Cards default to the `contrast` theme. It picks the dark theme furthest from the
theme around it, so a card always stands out without a hard flip to light. Each
row below is a Layout in the theme named on its cards.

- On `white` and `light`, the card becomes `dark-alt`.
- On `dark-alt` and `dark-plum`, the card becomes `dark`.
- On `dark`, the card becomes `dark-plum`.

Pass any other theme to the `theme` prop to opt out.

<Canvas of={CardStories.UseCaseContrast} />

In dark mode `white` and `light` become `dark-alt` and `dark-plum`, so the cards
step to `dark` rather than jumping to a light color.
